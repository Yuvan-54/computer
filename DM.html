Different Domains in Computer Science

1. Artificial Intelligence (AI)

AI focuses on building machines that can think and learn like humans.

2. Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity deals with protecting systems and data from cyber attacks.

3. Data Science

Data Science is about analyzing data to gain useful insights.

4. Software Engineering

This domain involves designing, developing, and maintaining software.

5. Networking

Networking covers how computers communicate and share data.
